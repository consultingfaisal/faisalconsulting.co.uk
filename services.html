Common questions

Is a Fractional Marketing Director the same as a Fractional CMO?

In practice, yes — the terms are used interchangeably in the UK market. I use Fractional Marketing Director because it's the clearer title, but the scope of work is the same senior, strategic role.

Does this work inside IR35?

Engagements are structured project and outcome-based, with control over how the work is delivered, which is how genuine outside-IR35 fractional work is typically structured. Your own status determination depends on your specific engagement, and I'd recommend checking it against HMRC's tool for your circumstances rather than relying on a general answer.

How long does an engagement typically last?

Fractional and interim work usually runs 3–6 months at minimum to see the impact through, though it varies by scope. Project work is often 2–8 weeks. Nothing is sold as a long minimum term.

Will you be dedicated to us, or working with other clients too?

Fractional and advisory work is designed to run alongside other clients — that's the point of the model. Interim engagements are typically full-time and dedicated to one business for the duration.

Can we use this alongside our existing marketing agency?

Yes, and it's a common setup — I often sit above the agency relationship, briefing and holding it accountable, rather than replacing it.

What happens if it's not the right fit?

We find that out on the discovery call, before anything is agreed. If it isn't a fit, I'll say so plainly rather than take on work that won't produce a result.

What happens on the diagnostic call

01

You describe the situation

What’s working, what isn’t, and what you’ve already tried. No prep needed — the conversation itself tends to surface the real problem quickly.

02

I ask the questions you haven’t been asked

Usually about tracking, attribution, agency accountability, or who actually owns the marketing outcome end-to-end. Most businesses have an answer to these questions — but haven’t been pushed to articulate it before.

03

We agree whether there’s a useful next step

No sales pitch, no follow-up pressure. If there’s a clear fit between what you need and what I do, I’ll say so plainly and suggest the right engagement type. If there isn’t, I’ll say that too.

Most calls take 15 minutes; I allow 30 minutes in the diary so we are not cut short.
