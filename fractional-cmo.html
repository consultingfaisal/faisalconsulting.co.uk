What a fractional CMO actually does

The job is the same as a full-time CMO's. The difference is the time commitment, not the seniority. In a typical engagement I take ownership of:

Marketing strategy and budget. Agreeing what marketing is accountable for commercially, then setting the plan and the spend to get there.
Measurement the board can trust. Tracking, attribution and reporting that show which pound is working, not just activity.
Team and agency leadership. Briefing, managing and holding in-house people and agencies accountable to the same outcome.
Board and investor reporting. A single, accountable voice for marketing in the room where decisions are made.
Hiring and handover. Building the function so it runs without me, including helping you hire a permanent marketing lead when the time is right.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in the UK?

Across the UK market, fractional CMO day rates run from a few hundred to well over a thousand pounds a day. What moves the number is seniority and track record, how many days a week you need, and whether the work is a focused fix or ongoing ownership of the whole function.

I don't publish a single day rate, because the fee is agreed to scope once I understand what's needed. The fairer comparison is total cost against the problem being solved: a permanent CMO brings salary, National Insurance, benefits, recruitment fees and notice periods, while a fractional CMO is priced against the actual gap. Common questions

What is a fractional CMO?

A Fractional CMO sits at the same strategic level as a full-time Chief Marketing Officer, owning the marketing function and accountable to the board, for a fraction of the commitment.

Is a fractional CMO the same as a fractional marketing director?

In practice, yes. "Fractional CMO" and "Fractional Marketing Director" describe the same seniority of role, and the title varies more by company culture than by the actual scope of work.

When should a business hire a fractional CMO?

When the business has outgrown ad hoc marketing but isn't ready for a permanent CMO salary, when the board wants a named, accountable owner for marketing performance rather than just an agency relationship, or when investors or the board are asking questions about marketing efficiency that nobody can currently answer with evidence.

How much time does a fractional CMO give, and for how long?

Fractional work is typically one to three days per week alongside your existing team, usually for three to six months at minimum to see the impact through. The fee is agreed to scope.

Can a fractional CMO work alongside our existing marketing agency?

Yes, and it is a common setup. I often sit above the agency relationship, briefing and holding it accountable, rather than replacing it.

How much does a fractional CMO cost in the UK?

Across the UK market, fractional CMO day rates run from a few hundred to well over a thousand pounds a day, depending on seniority, days per week and scope. I agree the fee to scope after a discovery call rather than publishing a single day rate.

What is the difference between a fractional CMO and an interim CMO?

A fractional CMO works part-time, typically one to three days a week, alongside your team over several months. An interim CMO or marketing director works full-time and is dedicated to one business for a fixed period, usually to cover a leadership gap.

How quickly can a fractional CMO start?

Usually within days of agreeing scope, rather than the two to six months a permanent CMO search tends to take.

Do you work with UK businesses outside London?

Yes. Most fractional CMO work runs remotely across the UK, with time on site for workshops, board meetings or team sessions when it adds value.
