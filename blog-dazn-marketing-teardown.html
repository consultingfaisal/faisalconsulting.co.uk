DAZN: what their public marketing signals are actually saying

19 September 2026 · Marketing Teardown

DAZN's Belgium help page currently offers a single plan: "DAZN Total — One plan. All the sport." No tiers, no comparing which sports sit in which package — one plan, with a free tier alongside it for anyone not ready to pay yet.

That's a genuine, substantive simplification. Not new packaging on the same confusing structure — an actual different structure.

Except it isn't live everywhere. Checked at the same time, DAZN's Switzerland and Austria help pages still show the old three-tier system — Super Sports, Unlimited, and Unlimited Plus — each bundling a different mix of sports at a different price.

Which means a fan doing what most fans actually do — reading a review, checking a forum, comparing prices across a friend's recommendation — has a real chance of encountering plan names and prices that don't match what they're actually offered once they land on their own country's page.

That single observation, and three others like it, are laid out in a one-page breakdown below — built entirely from what's publicly checkable: DAZN's own published help and pricing pages, compared directly across countries. Nothing here claims any insight into subscriber numbers, revenue, or internal reporting.

It isn't all critique, either. The Belgium page proves the team already knows exactly what "simple" should look like for this product — which changes what the actual fix is.
