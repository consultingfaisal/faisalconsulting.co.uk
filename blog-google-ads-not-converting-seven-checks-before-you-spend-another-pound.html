Google Ads Not Converting? Seven Checks Before You Spend Another Pound

10 November 2026

Many founders watch ad spend leave the account every month without seeing meaningful enquiries or sales. When Google ads are not converting, the instinct is often to rewrite the ad copy or raise the budget. That usually wastes more money.

Fix the foundations first. Here are seven checks to run before you spend another pound.

Check your conversion tracking

Faulty tracking makes campaigns look dead when they are actually working, or vice versa. Test your forms and phone lines today.

  • Submit a test lead on your site and confirm it fires in Google Analytics.
  • Check that phone number clicks and form submissions register as distinct conversion actions.
  • Review your account to ensure conversion values are set correctly if you use automated bidding.

Review your actual search terms

Keyword match types often pull in irrelevant traffic. Look at the search terms report rather than just your keyword list.

  • Export the search terms report for the last [FAISAL TO ADD] days.
  • Identify terms that cost money but have zero relevance to your actual offer.
  • Add negative keywords immediately to block irrelevant queries.

Evaluate the landing page

If the click is right but the page is weak, visitors will leave. Make sure the user journey makes sense.

  • Check that the headline matches the search term that brought the user there.
  • Remove friction from your primary contact or checkout form.
  • Ensure the page loads cleanly on mobile devices.

Next steps

If you run these checks and still find no clear answers, your entire setup may need an independent review. A structured marketing performance audit can identify hidden waste across your digital channels.
