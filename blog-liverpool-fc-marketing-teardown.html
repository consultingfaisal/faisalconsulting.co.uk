Liverpool FC: what their public marketing signals are actually saying

19 September 2026 · Marketing Teardown

Somewhere in liverpoolfc.com's own metadata sits a meta-keywords tag — on the club's "Report Abuse" page, of all places — that still lists Klopp, Mane and Firmino by name. A manager and two players who'd already left the club by the time this was checked.

Search engines barely weight that tag any more, so the direct visibility cost is small. The more interesting signal is what it implies: how long it's been since that particular template was actually looked at.

It isn't the only sign. A legacy.liverpoolfc.com domain is still live and indexed, running old news pages alongside the current site rather than redirecting cleanly into it — two versions of the club's own site, with no obvious signal to a fan or a search engine which one is current.

Neither of those is a crisis. But they're the kind of thing that only shows up once you actually look — and they sit alongside a genuinely interesting structural choice: membership sales that require joining before a hard cutoff, and a "Ticket Touting" page permanent enough to live in the main navigation, not just a one-off statement.
