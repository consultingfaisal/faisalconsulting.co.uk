Mars

Interim Performance Marketing Ops Director, 2021 to 2022

Rebuilt the UTM and campaign taxonomy framework across all 13 brands so tracking and attribution meant the same thing in every market. Used the resulting visibility to reallocate the 15-person team's effort and budget toward what the data actually showed was working.

75%+

reported year on year campaign efficiency improvement across 13 brands
