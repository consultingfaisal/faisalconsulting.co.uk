Quick answer: A Google Ads audit is an independent check of whether your account is spending money on the right searches, measuring the right conversions and sending people to pages that convert. I start with tracking, because bidding learns from the conversions you send it. You get a written list of findings in priority order, with the evidence for each one.

Signs your account needs an audit

Spend has gone up, but leads or sales have not.
Google Ads and Google Analytics report different conversion numbers.
Nobody has read the search terms report in the last month.
Your agency reports clicks and impressions, but not cost per lead or revenue.

What the audit checks

1. Conversion tracking. Which actions count as conversions, whether any are counted twice, and whether Google Ads and GA4 agree. More on that in why GA4 and Google Ads disagree on conversions.

2. Search terms and keywords. What people actually typed before they clicked, which terms waste money and which deserve their own keyword. See how to read the Google Ads search terms report.

3. Campaign structure and bidding. Whether the bid strategy suits the amount of conversion data the account has, and whether budgets sit where the results are.

4. Ads and assets. Whether the ads match what people searched for and what the page offers.

5. Landing pages. Speed, message match and whether the form or call to action works on mobile.

Common questions

What does a Google Ads audit cover?

Conversion tracking first, then search terms and keywords, campaign structure, bidding, ads and landing pages. Each finding says what is wrong, the evidence, what it costs and what to do about it.

Why check tracking first?

Smart bidding learns from the conversions you send it. If those conversions are wrong, missing or counted twice, every other change is built on bad data.

Is the audit independent?

Yes. I do not run your ads and I do not take a share of your media spend, so there is no reason to protect the current setup.

What access do you need?

Read access to Google Ads, Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager, and Search Console if you have it. No changes are made to the account during the audit.

What do I get at the end?

A written list of findings in priority order, with the evidence for each one and a clear next step. You can fix them in-house, hand them to your agency, or ask me to lead the work.
