Where is the problem showing up?

Your careers page and job ads are translated from Spanish, not written for an English-speaking candidate.
Your LinkedIn presence describes what the company does, but not what it is actually like to work there.
You post roles on job boards and hope, without knowing which channel brings applicants who actually stay.
International candidates drop out during the process because the experience shifts back to Spanish somewhere along the way.
You are competing for the same small pool of English-speaking talent as everyone else in your sector, with no clear reason to choose you.

This is not really a translation problem, and it is not solved by posting the same job ad on more sites. It is a marketing problem — visibility, a clear story, and a way to measure what is actually working — applied to attracting people instead of customers.
