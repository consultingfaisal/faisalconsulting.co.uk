Quick answer: A fractional marketing director is a senior marketing leader who owns your marketing function part-time, typically one to three days a week. It suits businesses that have outgrown ad hoc marketing but do not need, or cannot yet justify, a permanent marketing director. Engagements usually run three to six months, with the fee agreed to scope.

When a fractional marketing director is the right fit

The business has outgrown ad hoc marketing, but a permanent director salary is hard to justify.
Marketing spend is growing, and nobody can say with evidence which part of it is working.
Agencies and freelancers are busy, but nobody senior is holding them to one commercial outcome.
The board or investors want a named, accountable owner for marketing performance.

What I take ownership of

Strategy and budget. Agreeing what marketing is accountable for commercially, then setting the plan and the spend to get there.

Measurement you can trust. Tracking, attribution and reporting that show which channel and campaign is paying back. A marketing performance audit is usually the first step.

Team and agency leadership. Briefing and managing in-house people and agencies against the same outcome.

Reporting to the board. One accountable voice for marketing where decisions are made.

Hiring and handover. Building the function so it runs without me, including helping you hire a permanent marketing lead when the time is right.

Fractional, interim, full-time or agency?

A fractional marketing director works part-time and owns the outcome. A full-time director brings salary, National Insurance, benefits, recruitment fees and notice periods, and usually takes months to hire. An interim marketing director is full-time for a fixed period, which suits a sudden leadership gap. An agency executes what it is briefed, but does not own your commercial outcome.

'Fractional marketing director' and 'fractional CMO' describe the same level of role. For what it costs and how it is contracted, see how much a fractional marketing director costs and IR35 and fractional marketing.

Common questions

What is a fractional marketing director?

A fractional marketing director is a senior marketing leader who owns your marketing function part-time, typically one to three days a week. You get director-level strategy, budget ownership and team leadership without the cost of a full-time hire.

Is a fractional marketing director the same as a fractional CMO?

They describe the same seniority of role. The title varies more by company culture than by the actual scope of the work.

How many days a week does a fractional marketing director work?

Typically one to three days a week, agreed to the scope of the work. Most engagements run for three to six months, with the fee agreed to scope.

How is it different from an interim marketing director?

Interim work is full-time and dedicated to one business for a fixed period. Fractional work is part-time and runs alongside your existing team.

Do you work with UK businesses from Spain?

Yes. I work remotely with UK and European businesses, with time on site when it matters. Meetings, reporting and agency management run over video and in shared documents.
