Mars

Interim Performance Marketing Ops Director, 2021 to 2022

Mars needed one person owning performance marketing across 13 brands and 4 European markets, with a 15-person team spread across four countries and a £75M+ budget. I rebuilt the campaign taxonomy and tracking framework so spend could be compared properly across markets for the first time, then used that visibility to reallocate budget toward what was actually working.

75%+

year-on-year efficiency gain

Tata Communications

Associate Director, Performance Marketing & Media Planning, 2017 to 2019

Digital was 1% of Tata Communications' £2bn sales and marketing contribution when I joined — treated as an experiment, not a channel. I rebuilt the strategy, the team, and the measurement behind it. Two years later digital's contribution had grown to 20% of the portfolio, on only 100% budget growth: a 20x revenue outcome on 2x spend. The work also won a 2018 ITSMA Global Award for automated ABM campaigns.

£450K → £9M

digital contribution, in two years
