What a marketing performance audit covers

The starting point is not another dashboard. It is a clear view of what the business is trying to achieve, which evidence can be trusted and where performance is being lost.

Measurement and tracking

I check whether the events, conversions and revenue signals used in reporting are complete, consistent and useful for decision making.

Paid media and acquisition

I review campaign structure, search terms, bidding signals, landing pages and the difference between reported platform return and commercial return.

Agency and team accountability

I reconcile what has been reported against the underlying evidence, clarify who owns each outcome and identify decisions that have been left unresolved.

Priorities and next actions

You get a practical order of work. The first actions are the ones that restore confidence in the numbers or remove the largest avoidable loss.
