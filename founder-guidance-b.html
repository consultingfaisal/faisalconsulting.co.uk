Where is the problem showing up?

Not sure your pricing actually covers your real costs, let alone leaves a margin?
Product or menu range has grown organically, with no one testing what actually sells?
Managing stock by feel rather than a system, so you're overordering or running out?
Brand look and feel inconsistent across social, packaging and in person, with no one owning it?
Making decisions on gut feel because there's no one who's done this before to ask?

None of this is unusual at this stage — almost every early business hits some version of it. But it's exactly where a bit of experienced guidance, early, saves you the most time, money and stress later.
