Why your Costa Blanca property listings don't convert English buyers

18 September 2026

Most Costa Blanca agencies selling to British buyers have already done the obvious things: photos, a translated listing, maybe an English-speaking agent. And the enquiries still don't convert at the rate they should. The gap usually isn't visible in the listing itself — it's in what happens around it.

Translated isn't the same as written for the buyer

A literal translation of a Spanish listing reads like exactly that. British buyers researching a property from home are used to a specific format — distances to the airport in minutes, not kilometres; running costs and community fees stated plainly; a clear answer to "can I actually rent this out." Listings that skip these read as incomplete, not just foreign.

Search behaviour is genuinely different by language

A property that ranks well for Spanish search terms often has close to no visibility for the English equivalents, because they're different searches with different intent — "chalet Alicante" and "villa Costa Blanca for sale" are not the same query with a different word for the same thing. Treating them as one page's job to cover is why visibility for one language doesn't carry over to the other.

The buying timeline is longer, and most follow-up doesn't survive it

International buyers frequently take months between first enquiry and offer — researching remotely, planning a viewing trip, sorting finance and residency questions before committing. A lead that isn't followed up systematically across that whole window looks cold well before the buyer has actually decided against the purchase.

Reviews and enquiries in English often wait longer for a reply

Directory research into this exact market turns up the same pattern repeatedly: English-language reviews and enquiries left unanswered for days, while Spanish ones get a same-day response — not from unwillingness, usually just an operational gap nobody had scheduled for.

What actually closes the gap

Not a bigger budget on the same approach — a measurement system that shows which listings, channels and follow-up steps are actually producing English-speaking buyers, so the fix targets the real leak rather than a guess. That's the starting point covered on the Costa Blanca & Valencia page.
