Where is the problem showing up?

Your website, listings or brochure read like they were translated, not written for an English speaker.
You rank well in Spanish search, but barely appear when someone searches in English for the same thing.
Google reviews and enquiries in English go unanswered for days, while Spanish ones get a same-day reply.
You cannot tell whether a lead or sale came from a local, a British buyer, a resident expat or a returning visitor.
Marketing activity follows the Spanish calendar, but misses the UK school holidays, viewing seasons or renewal periods that actually drive your international customers.

When several of these happen together, the answer is rarely just "do more marketing in English." It's usually one system serving two audiences properly, with evidence to show which parts are actually working — not a guess translated from what works locally.
